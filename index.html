Finance Student, Xavier University

Financial markets reward whoever reads uncertainty first, and that challenge is what pulled me into finance. What holds my attention most is not just the data behind a price move, but the intersection of communicators: the analysts, economists, and cross-cultural voices whose words decide how people act on that data. As a first generation college student with working French and Italian, and connections spanning five countries, I read that intersection through a multicultural lens most of my peers do not have. I look for what a working analyst looks for: evaluate the information, decide under pressure, and understand how that decision moves a person or a business toward its goal.

Outside the classroom, the situations that test judgment fastest are rarely dramatic: a delayed shipment, a problem worth solving before it grows into a larger one. Those moments taught me to take initiative and stay level headed when the stakes rise. The same instincts, thinking clearly, communicating directly, and following through, are what the work in finance actually asks for.

Voices I Follow

The people who move markets are usually the ones who can explain them well. I follow Peter Diamandis' Moonshots podcast for exactly that reason: hearing entrepreneurs and industry leaders explain their own thinking teaches me more about how ideas travel from a boardroom to a balance sheet than any textbook could. Markets do not just move on data. They move on the people communicating what the data means, across languages and across cultures, and that intersection is where I spend most of my attention.

Where I Add Value

  • A first generation perspective that treats financial literacy as something worth building, not assumed
  • Working French and Italian, plus business connections across five countries, brought to how I read cross-cultural markets
  • Selected for the Xavier NEXT AI at Work cohort, studying how artificial intelligence is changing finance and organizations under Professor Jarrod Humphrey
  • Real experience turning a brand's voice into engagement, the same skill markets need from anyone explaining what the numbers mean
