Finance Student | Xavier University

I chose to study finance because I have always been fascinated by the uncertainty of financial markets and the challenge of understanding where prices may move next. The combination of data, human behavior, and risk makes finance a field that constantly rewards curiosity and analytical thinking. I enjoy evaluating information, making decisions under pressure, and learning how financial professionals help individuals and businesses achieve their goals.

Outside the classroom, I have developed strong problem-solving skills through handling time-sensitive situations where quick action was necessary. Whether tracking down delayed packages or finding solutions before issues became larger problems, I learned the importance of taking initiative and staying calm when the stakes are high. These experiences strengthened my ability to think critically, communicate effectively, and follow through on responsibilities.

Interests

In my free time, I enjoy listening to Peter Diamandis' Moonshots podcast. Hearing conversations with innovators, entrepreneurs, and industry leaders has expanded my perspective on technology, business, and the future. I am especially interested in how breakthrough ideas are turned into real-world solutions and how innovation continues to transform markets and industries.
